## Overview

PrivacyMedic provides data privacy governance and AI privacy services: GenAI data governance, AI training data lineage, NIST Privacy Framework implementation, GDPR/CCPA/state privacy law compliance. Privacy is distinct from security — it focuses on data minimization, transparency, individual rights, and lawful data processing.

**Market Position:** Privacy-first governance for organizations navigating AI data risks, state privacy laws, and NIST Privacy Framework adoption. Framework-driven. Vendor-neutral. Practical privacy programs, not legal opinions.

**Target Audiences:** SMB (employee AI usage, state privacy law compliance), private enterprise (AI training data risk, multi-state privacy obligations), public sector (constituent data, transparency), K-12 (student data in AI tools, FERPA privacy).

### 4. NIST Privacy Framework Implementation
**Description:** End-to-end privacy program build aligned to NIST Privacy Framework (5 Functions: Identify-P, Govern-P, Control-P, Communicate-P, Protect-P). Parallel structure to NIST CSF, but privacy-focused.

**Deliverables:**
- Privacy risk assessment (NIST Privacy Framework Identify-P)
- Privacy governance structure (roles, accountability, policies — Govern-P)
- Data lifecycle controls (collection minimization, retention, deletion — Control-P)
- Privacy notice and transparency program (Communicate-P)
- Technical privacy protections (encryption, access controls, de-identification — Protect-P)
- Privacy Framework Profile (current state + target state)
- Privacy program roadmap (12–24 months)

**Audience Fit:**
- **SMB** — Privacy compliance beyond just security (HIPAA Privacy Rule, state laws)
- **Private Enterprise** — Privacy risk assessment, DPO/CPO support, privacy-by-design programs
- **Public Sector** — Privacy programs for SLED entities (constituent data, FOIL/FOIA considerations)
- **K-12 Secondary** — Student privacy program aligned to NIST (FERPA + NIST Privacy Framework)

**Pack Path:** `packs/privacymedic/nist-privacy-framework/<audience>.md`

**Description:** Multi-jurisdiction privacy law compliance: GDPR (EU), CCPA/CPRA (California), Virginia CDPA, Colorado CPA, Connecticut CTDPA, and emerging state laws. Includes data mapping, privacy notices, individual rights processes (access, deletion, opt-out), vendor contracts (DPAs).

**Deliverables:**

- Privacy law compliance matrix (which laws apply, what they require, current compliance status)
- Records of Processing Activities (GDPR Article 30, CCPA record-keeping)

**Audience Fit:**
- **SMB** — Multi-state operations, e-commerce, SaaS, web apps collecting consumer data
- **Private Enterprise** — Cross-border data flows, EU customers, multi-state privacy obligations

## Typical Engagement Model

1. **Discovery Call:** 30–60 minutes. Identify privacy trigger (AI privacy concern, state law requirement, GDPR compliance, customer contract). Understand data flows and business model.
2. **Proposal:** Fixed-price project (30–90 days for most privacy programs) or ongoing retainer (for DPO services).
3. **Kickoff:** Week 1. Data mapping, stakeholder interviews, system/vendor inventory.
4. **Execution:** Weeks 2–8. Privacy risk assessment, policy development, vendor contract review.
5. **Delivery:** Week 8–12. Privacy program documentation, notices, training materials. Client review.
6. **Ongoing (optional):** Annual privacy program reviews, data subject request support, privacy audit prep.

**Note:** Privacy programs often require legal counsel for contract negotiation and regulatory interpretation. PrivacyMedic provides privacy governance frameworks and operational guidance, not legal advice. Clients should consult their attorney for legal questions.

## Notes

- Privacy and security overlap but are distinct disciplines. Security protects data from unauthorized access. Privacy governs lawful, transparent, minimized data use.
- PrivacyMedic does NOT provide legal advice. Clients must consult their attorney for contract negotiation, regulatory interpretation, and legal risk decisions.
- AI privacy is the fastest-growing area. GenAI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Midjourney) are ubiquitous, and most orgs have no governance around them.
- NIST Privacy Framework is less well-known than NIST CSF, but it's gaining traction (especially in public sector and regulated industries).
- GDPR and CCPA are mature. Emerging state laws (Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, etc.) are creating multi-state compliance complexity. Data mapping is the foundation for all privacy compliance.
