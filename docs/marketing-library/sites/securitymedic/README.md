## Overview

Security Medic is the flagship hub site for all Security Medic services. It serves as the primary entry point for new prospects, provides a service catalog overview, and directs visitors to specialized sub-brands (Hudson Valley CISO, CyberIntelPro, PrivacyMedic).

**Market Position:** Practical, framework-first cybersecurity and compliance for Hudson Valley / New York SMBs, regulated organizations, and MSPs.

**Target Audiences:** All five (SMB, private enterprise, public sector, K-12, MSPs) — with SMB as the primary focus.

---

## Services Offered

### 1. Free Security Assessment
**Description:** Entry-point offer. 30–60 minute consultation + lightweight risk assessment. Identifies top 3 gaps and provides a roadmap for remediation. No obligation.

**Audience Fit:**
- **SMB** — Insurance questionnaire prep, compliance readiness check
- **Private Enterprise** — M&A due diligence prep, AI governance scoping
- **Public Sector** — Grant application support, mandate compliance scoping
- **K-12 Secondary** — FERPA program gap analysis
- **MSPs** — Partner pipeline builder, client risk assessment for MSP clients

**Pack Path:** `packs/securitymedic/free-security-assessment/<audience>.md`

**Audience Fit:**
- **SMB** — Shadow AI sprawl, employee AI usage policy
- **Private Enterprise** — Board-level AI governance, model risk frameworks
- **MSPs** — White-label AI security service offering for MSP clients

**Pack Path:** `packs/securitymedic/ai-security-pillars/<audience>.md`

**Audience Fit:**
- **MSPs only** — Partner program for MSPs needing CISO-level expertise to support their clients

## Typical Customer Journey

1. **Awareness:** Prospect Googles "HIPAA compliance consultant Hudson Valley" or "fractional CISO New York" → lands on Security Medic blog post or service page.
2. **Engagement:** Prospect fills out "Free Security Assessment" form or contacts via email.
3. **Discovery:** Jim or agent conducts 30–60 minute consultation. Identifies trigger (insurance, audit, contract requirement). Qualifies budget and timeline.
4. **Routing:** Based on need, prospect is directed to:
   - **Hudson Valley CISO** for ongoing fractional CISO services
   - **CyberIntelPro** for project-based work (Shadow AI sprint, CSF deployment)
   - **PrivacyMedic** for AI privacy or data privacy programs
   - **Security Medic compliance packages** for turnkey HIPAA/NYDFS/SOC 2 programs
5. **Proposal:** One-pager + pricing sent. SMBs decide quickly. Enterprises take 2–4 weeks.
6. **Close:** SOW signed, kickoff scheduled.
