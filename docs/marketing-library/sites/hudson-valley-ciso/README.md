## Overview

Hudson Valley CISO provides fractional (part-time) CISO services for organizations that need executive-level security leadership but cannot justify or afford a full-time CISO hire. Jim Venuto serves as the fractional CISO, providing strategic governance, board reporting, compliance oversight, and program leadership.

**Market Position:** Strategic security leadership for Hudson Valley and New York organizations. Framework-first, board-ready, compliance-focused.

**Target Audiences:** SMB (insurance-driven, compliance-mandated), private enterprise (M&A prep, board governance), public sector (budget-constrained municipalities), K-12 (superintendent + board support), MSPs (co-delivery partner for MSP clients).

## Service Tiers

Hudson Valley CISO offers three fractional CISO tiers, scaled by engagement level and organizational complexity:

### Tier 1: Part-Time CISO (8–16 hours/month)
**Description:** Policy development, risk assessment, compliance roadmap, quarterly check-ins. Ideal for SMBs and budget-constrained public sector entities that need CISO-level guidance but not active program management.

**Deliverables:**
- 12-month security roadmap aligned to NIST CSF 2.0 or applicable framework
- Core security policies (incident response, acceptable use, data protection, vendor management)
- Risk assessment (identify, analyze, prioritize risks)
- Quarterly board/executive briefing (one-page risk summary)
- Annual review and roadmap update

**Audience Fit:**
- **SMB** — First-time CISO leadership, insurance/compliance-driven
- **Public Sector** — Small municipalities, towns, counties with limited IT staff
- **K-12 Secondary** — Districts needing superintendent + board support

**Pack Path:** `packs/hudson-valley-ciso/fractional-ciso-tier-1/<audience>.md`

---

### Tier 2: Active CISO (16–32 hours/month)
**Description:** Hands-on program management, vendor oversight, audit coordination, monthly board reporting. Ideal for mid-sized organizations with active compliance obligations or multi-site operations.

**Deliverables:**
- All Tier 1 deliverables PLUS:
- Active vendor risk program (third-party assessments, contract review, ongoing monitoring)
- Audit coordination (SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, NYDFS) — gap remediation, evidence collection, auditor liaison
- Monthly board/executive reporting with KPIs and risk trends
- Incident response leadership (not 24/7 SOC, but breach coordination and post-incident review)
- Staff training and awareness programs (quarterly phishing tests, annual security training)

**Audience Fit:**
- **SMB** — Multi-site orgs, regulated industries, active compliance requirements
- **Private Enterprise** — Mid-sized companies, M&A preparation, board risk committee support
- **Public Sector** — County governments, regional service districts, larger municipalities

**Pack Path:** `packs/hudson-valley-ciso/fractional-ciso-tier-2/<audience>.md`

---

### Tier 3: Strategic CISO (32–40 hours/month)
**Description:** Executive-level leadership, board risk committee participation, cross-functional program governance, M&A due diligence oversight. Ideal for large private enterprises or organizations with complex risk profiles.

**Deliverables:**
- All Tier 2 deliverables PLUS:
- Board risk committee participation (attend meetings, present risk reports, advise on strategic risk decisions)
- M&A due diligence leadership (buyer or seller side — security posture assessment, gap remediation, integration planning)
- Multi-framework harmonization (SOC 2 + ISO 27001 + NIST CSF unified control framework)
- Executive risk communication (CFO, General Counsel, C-suite briefings)
- Strategic vendor negotiations (security terms in major contracts)

**Audience Fit:**
- **Private Enterprise** — Growth-stage companies, pre-IPO, M&A-active orgs
- **Public Sector** — State agencies, large counties, regional authorities

**Pack Path:** `packs/hudson-valley-ciso/fractional-ciso-tier-3/<audience>.md`

## Typical Engagement Model

1. **Discovery Call:** 30–60 minutes. Identify trigger (insurance, audit, M&A, board requirement). Understand org size, complexity, current state.
2. **Tier Recommendation:** Based on org needs, recommend Tier 1 (roadmap + policy), Tier 2 (active management), or Tier 3 (strategic leadership).
3. **Proposal:** One-pager with scope, deliverables, timeline, pricing. Fixed monthly retainer (not hourly).
4. **Kickoff:** First month: risk assessment, stakeholder interviews, gap identification.
5. **Ongoing:** Monthly/quarterly deliverables. Board reports. Policy updates. Audit support as needed.
6. **Annual Review:** Roadmap refresh, pricing review, tier adjustment if scope has changed.
