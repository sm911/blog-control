## One-Pager

### Problem

Your clients are asking questions your help desk was never staffed to answer: cyber insurance questionnaires, NYDFS / HIPAA / SOC 2 expectations, “who is your CISO?”, shadow AI, board risk reports. Saying “we patch and monitor” no longer closes renewals. Hiring a full-time CISO destroys margin. Ignoring the gap risks losing the account to a competitor who shows up with compliance language.

You need a **co-delivery** model: you keep the client relationship and operations; Security Medic adds fractional CISO / compliance depth you can introduce with confidence.

### Solution

Security Medic’s **MSP Partnership Program** gives MSP principals a white-label / co-branded path to offer vCISO and compliance support without building a security practice from scratch.

**What partners get:**
- Clear intro script for MSP principals (how to raise compliance without scaring the client)
- Co-delivery model: MSP = operations; Security Medic = governance, risk, compliance packaging
- Access to fractional CISO tiers via [hudsonvalleyciso.com](https://hudsonvalleyciso.com)
- Project lanes via [cyberintelpro.com](https://cyberintelpro.com) (e.g., Shadow AI sprint, CSF ops)
- Privacy lane via [privacymedic.com](https://privacymedic.com) when GenAI / student / constituent data is in play
- Soft entry: [Free Security Assessment](https://securitymedic.com) as a joint discovery offer

**What this is not:** We do not replace your NOC/help desk, sell a fake SOC under your brand, or take over the client relationship.

### Proof

Framework-first (NIST CSF 2.0, HIPAA, NYDFS Part 500 where applicable), vendor-neutral, Hudson Valley / NY grounded. No invented win rates or fake MSP case studies. Positioning is co-delivery and margin protection — not “outsource everything.”

### Next Step

MSP principals: start with a partner intro call via [securitymedic.com](https://securitymedic.com). Bring one client trigger (insurance renewal, questionnaire, audit ask) and we’ll map the co-delivery path.

## Talk Track

### Opening (30 seconds)

“I’m not here to take your clients. I’m here so when they ask for a CISO, an insurance evidence pack, or an AI usage policy, you have a partner who shows up under your introduction — and you stay the trusted MSP.”

### Discovery Questions (3–5 key questions)

1. **“Which client conversations are you losing or stalling on — insurance, compliance questionnaires, AI, or board asks?”**
2. **“Do you want white-label deliverables, co-branded, or a warm handoff to Hudson Valley CISO under your intro?”**
3. **“Who owns the relationship day-to-day — you as principal, or an account manager?”**
4. **“Any NYDFS / healthcare / education clients where regulation is explicit?”**
5. **“What’s your comfort level bringing a fractional CISO into QBRs?”**

### Pitch (2–3 minutes)

“Here’s the model. You keep break/fix, patching, and the day-to-day trust. We add the compliance and governance layer: policies, risk assessment language, insurance evidence coaching, AI governance when shadow AI shows up. Clients hear one story: their MSP brought senior help, not a bait-and-switch.

Entry is usually a joint Free Security Assessment or a single client workshop. If it fits, we move to a fractional retainer or a fixed project — you stay in the loop on QBRs. Pricing is structured so you’re not eating CISO salary on your P&L.”

### Objection Handling

- **“You’ll steal my clients.”** → “Contract and operating model keep you as relationship owner. We don’t bid your book. Intro is always through you.”
- **“My techs can do compliance.”** → “Techs keep the stack healthy. Compliance needs named governance, documentation, and board language — different skill. We fill that gap so your techs aren’t fake-CISOs after hours.”
- **“Margins are already thin.”** → “That’s why this is co-delivery, not hiring. We aim for packages you can attach or refer with clear scope — not open-ended bleed.”
- **“We already have a security vendor.”** → “Great — if they cover governance and insurance evidence, you’re set. If they only sell tools or MDR, we complement; we don’t pitch competing agents.”
- **“Prove it.”** → “We won’t invent case studies. We’ll walk one live client trigger with you — questionnaire or renewal — and show the deliverable shape before you commit.”

### Close

“Pick one client with a real deadline. We run a joint discovery, you stay on the call, and you decide if the partnership model fits. I’ll send the one-pager and intro script today.”
